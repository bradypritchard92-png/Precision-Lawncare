My services

Everything I offer is hands-on work I do myself — so quality stays consistent and you always know who to call.

✂️

Lawn mowing

Regular mowing keeps your lawn healthy and looking cared for. I cut at a sensible height for the season, follow a consistent pattern, and bag or mulch based on what you prefer.

  • Weekly or bi-weekly schedules
  • Careful around beds, trees, and obstacles
  • Clippings handled the way you prefer

I treat every lawn like a neighbour’s yard — because it is. You’ll get a clean cut, not a rushed pass. If weather delays a visit, I’ll let you know and reschedule promptly.

Ask about mowing

📐

Edging and trimming

Sharp edges make the whole property look finished. I edge along sidewalks, driveways, and garden beds, and trim where the mower can’t reach.

  • Clean lines along hard surfaces
  • Trimming around fences, posts, and plantings
  • Available with mowing or as a standalone tidy-up

This is the detail work that separates “okay” from “looks great.” I take my time so your borders stay crisp between visits.

Ask about edging

🍂

Spring and fall cleanup

Seasonal cleanups reset your yard — clearing winter debris in spring, and prepping beds and lawns before the cold sets in each fall.

  • Debris and stick removal
  • Bed tidy-ups and general yard clearing
  • Custom scope based on your property

I’ll walk the property with you (or by photos) so the quote matches what you actually need — no surprise extras, and no corners left messy.

Ask about cleanup

🍁

Leaf removal

Heavy leaf cover can smother grass and clog beds. I clear leaves thoroughly and haul them away or leave them bagged as you prefer.

  • Full lawn and bed clearing
  • One-time or multi-visit leaf season plans
  • Disposal options discussed up front

Autumn can be busy — book early if you want regular leaf visits so your lawn isn’t buried under a week of fall.

Ask about leaf removal

Hedge trimming

I trim hedges for a neat, even shape that suits your home — not over-cut, not left ragged. Suited to common residential hedges and shrubs.

  • Shape maintenance and seasonal trims
  • Cleanup of clippings included
  • Honest advice if a plant needs more than a trim

If your hedges are overdue or you’ve taken them on yourself and want a reset, I’m happy to take a look and quote fairly.

Snow / winter services

When snow hits, you need a clear path out. I offer driveway and walkway clearing for residential properties in my service area.

  • Driveway and walkway clearing
  • Seasonal arrangements available
  • Prompt service after storms when booked

Winter slots fill up — reach out before the first big dump if you’d like me on your list for the season.

Gutter cleaning

Clogged gutters lead to overflow, staining, and foundation issues. I clear gutters and check that downspouts are flowing freely.

  • Gutter and downspout clearing
  • Debris removed from the site
  • Ideal paired with fall cleanup

I only take on jobs I’m comfortable completing safely. If your roof setup isn’t a fit, I’ll tell you straight.

Ask about gutters

🌾

Dethatching

Thatch is the layer of dead grass between soil and green growth. Too much of it blocks water and air. I dethatch when your lawn needs it — typically in the right season for your grass type.

  • Mechanical dethatching for residential lawns
  • Debris collected and removed
  • Advice on whether your lawn actually needs it

Not every lawn needs dethatching every year. I’ll assess first so you only pay for work that helps.

Ask about dethatching
